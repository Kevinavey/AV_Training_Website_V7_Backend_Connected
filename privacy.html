Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 September 2026

AV Training & Consultancy Limited respects your privacy and handles personal information only where needed to provide training, process bookings and payments, communicate with learners and meet our legal and administrative obligations.

Information we collect

When you make an enquiry or booking, we may collect information such as your name, email address, telephone number, course selection, delegate details, accessibility or booking information you choose to provide, payment status and booking reference.

Payments

Card payments are processed securely by Stripe. AV Training & Consultancy does not store your full card details on this website.

How we use your information

We use personal information to administer bookings, provide course and joining information, communicate about your training, maintain appropriate training and business records, prevent misuse of our services and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Sharing information

We may use trusted service providers where necessary to operate our booking, payment, email, calendar and website systems. We do not sell your personal information.

Retention and security

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected and for applicable legal, accounting, training or record-keeping requirements. We take reasonable measures to protect the information we hold.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and data portability, depending on the circumstances. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office if you have concerns about how your personal information is handled.

Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, contact info@avtrainingandconsultancy.co.uk.
