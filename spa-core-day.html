What you'll learn

Core workplace health and safety knowledge.

The course introduces the key principles needed to recognise hazards, understand workplace responsibilities and make safer decisions at work.

Topics include organising for safety, workplace hazards, tools and machinery, occupational health, emergency procedures and environmental responsibilities.

ECS route

SPA Core Day and the ECS Electrical Labourer Card

ECS currently lists the Safety Passport Alliance (SPA) Core H&S Course among the approved health and safety routes for Electrical Labourer applicants.

If an eligible SPA Core H&S course was completed within the previous three years, ECS guidance also provides an exemption from the separate ECS Health, Safety & Environmental Assessment.

Important: SPA Core Day does not by itself guarantee an ECS card. You must satisfy the other ECS requirements applicable to your route.

FAQ

SPA Core Day questions.

What is the SPA Core Day course?

SPA Core Day is a one-day health and safety course covering core workplace safety topics and forming part of the Safety Pass Alliance passport scheme.

Can SPA Core Day be used for an ECS Electrical Labourer Card application?

ECS lists the Safety Passport Alliance Core H&S Course among approved health and safety routes. If the eligible SPA course was completed within the past three years, it can also provide an exemption from the separate ECS Health, Safety & Environmental Assessment. Other ECS card requirements still apply.

Can I take SPA Core Day online?

Yes. AV Training & Consultancy offers live online delivery as well as classroom training.

How much is the course?

The advertised course fee is £154.
