About the course

About the SPA Health & Safety Core Day

The SPA Health & Safety Core Day is a one-day training course delivered live online via Microsoft Teams or through classroom training. Priced at £154.00, it provides essential health and safety knowledge for anyone working in environments where safety is a priority.

We run the course regularly throughout the year, with additional dates and group bookings available on request.

Developed by the Safety Pass Alliance (SPA), this nationally recognised course is suitable for people across a wide range of industries, including construction, engineering, manufacturing, utilities and electrical work.

For those looking to obtain an ECS Labourer Card, the SPA Core Day helps meet the health and safety requirement for eligible applications, making it one of the quickest recognised routes into the electrical industry.

Why choose this course?

By the end of the day, you will have the knowledge and confidence to recognise workplace hazards and work safely in a variety of environments.

After successfully completing the course, you will receive the SPA Health & Safety Passport, a nationally recognised qualification that demonstrates your health and safety awareness.

For candidates applying for or renewing an ECS Labourer Card, the SPA Passport can support the health and safety requirement, subject to current ECS eligibility criteria.

What you'll learn

Six key areas of workplace health and safety.

01

Organising for Safety

Legal responsibilities, vulnerable workers and enforcing authorities.

02

The Workplace

Hazards, safe working environments, working at height and asbestos awareness.

03

Tools, Plant & Machinery

Safe use of hand tools, power tools, machinery, plant and workplace vehicles.

04

Health

Hazardous substances, manual handling, noise, vibration and workplace stress.

05

Procedures

Emergency arrangements, accident reporting, safe systems and first aid principles.

06

The Environment

Waste management, environmental responsibilities and incident response.

FAQ

Questions learners ask.

Is the course online or classroom?

Both options are available. You can choose live online training via Microsoft Teams or classroom training at West Thames College.

Does this guarantee my ECS card?

No course can guarantee an ECS card. SPA Core Day can help eligible applicants meet the Health & Safety requirement, but candidates must also satisfy the current ECS eligibility criteria.
